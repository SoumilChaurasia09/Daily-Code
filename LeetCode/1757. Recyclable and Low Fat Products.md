# 1757. Recyclable and Low Fat Products

**Problem Name:** Recyclable and Low Fat Products
**Difficulty:** Easy

---

## Question

Find the `product_id` of products that are:

* Low fat → `low_fats = 'Y'`
* Recyclable → `recyclable = 'Y'`

Both conditions must be true.

---

## My Solution

```sql
SELECT product_id
FROM Products
WHERE low_fats = 'Y'
AND recyclable = 'Y';
```

---

## Approach

Select the `product_id` from the `Products` table.

We use `WHERE` to filter products that satisfy both conditions:

```sql
WHERE low_fats = 'Y'
AND recyclable = 'Y'
```

The `AND` operator means both conditions must be true.

---

## Example

Given:

```text
product_id | low_fats | recyclable
-----------|----------|-----------
0          | Y        | N
1          | Y        | Y
2          | N        | Y
3          | Y        | Y
4          | N        | N
```

Products `1` and `3` satisfy both conditions.

Therefore:

```text
1
3
```

---

## Key Takeaway

When multiple conditions must be satisfied, use `AND`:

```sql
WHERE condition1
AND condition2;
```

Here:

```sql
low_fats = 'Y'
AND recyclable = 'Y'
```
